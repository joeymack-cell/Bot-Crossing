# Bot Crossing Desktop v1.0.0

First shareable Windows desktop edition of this Bot Crossing fork.

- Built-in Codex chat with resizable panels, drafts, approvals, and stop controls.
- Session buildings with matching section and face colors.
- Recent-activity suit colors and adjustable history filters.
- Individual backpacks and expressions, greetings, and faster mini helpers.
- Physical headquarters lettering and improved city navigation.
- Full graphics controls and optional scoped host integration.

Download Bot-Crossing-Windows-v1.0.0.zip, extract the entire folder, and open Bot Crossing.exe. Requires Windows x64 and your own installed/signed-in Codex app for local sessions and chat. The portable app is unsigned. Watching is local; sending chat uses your Codex account.

This is a fork of Jarren Rocks' Bot Crossing. Original MIT and bundled third-party notices are preserved. The archive contains no personal sessions or saved settings.
